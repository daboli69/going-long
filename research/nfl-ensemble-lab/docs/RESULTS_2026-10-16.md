# Phase 1 results: model version v20261016_203729 (2026-10-16)

Real run on 2,841 completed games (2016 through 2026 week 6), 78 features. All numbers are
out-of-sample walk-forward results copied from `artifacts/runs/v20261016_203729/`.
The research champion was chosen on 2023–2024 before 2025 was scored.

Findings:
- No model or ensemble beats the closing market in any split. The gap on 2025 is about
  0.03 log loss.
- The champion margin on validation is tiny (0.629 vs 0.631–0.635). On the 2025 test it was
  one of the weaker models and the worst calibrated (ECE 0.089). The ranking among the top
  models is noise-level.
- The ensembles did not beat the best single model on validation. On test, the equal and
  weighted averages had the best log loss (0.634) by a negligible margin.
- A one-step point-differential logistic baseline is about as good as the full models on
  2025 (0.636). Most of the signal is "who has been better", and it is already priced in.
- 2026 (79 games) intervals are very wide (±10 points of accuracy). Do not read anything into them yet.

## Known limitations
- Lines are untimestamped closing consensus: a benchmark only. No betting-return backtest was run,
  and none of these numbers say anything about profit.
- The single-layer network's selected weight decay (0.3) sits at the edge of its grid.
- Development-split rows for 2020–2022 overlap with tuning and are optimistic; they are excluded above.
- Historical starting-QB identity is the recorded starter (small hindsight risk for late scratches).
