# Fixed-decision QB availability study, 2026-10-26

Status: feasibility audit first; at most one candidate, no historical holdout reopening. This study is distinct from the uncommitted Phase 16 line-movement proposal. No source collection, scheduler changes, promotion or push.

## Question and evidence

Does verified QB availability add winner-probability information to frozen champion v20261016_212540? One row per game at scheduled kickoff minus 90 minutes. Select the latest original champion snapshot at or before that time, never a later stage. Preserve its age, full-file SHA-256, model manifest/bundle hashes, source timestamps and missingness. Do not treat a source's latest revised file as a historical snapshot. A hash computed today authenticates current bytes, not historical publication.

Use only local original archives. Inspect archived ESPN injury reports, nflverse injury revisions, phase7 source revisions and depth-chart provenance. A depth rank or projected starter is not confirmed availability. QB evidence must identify the champion-assumed QB and verified available replacement by stable ID, with original source evidence and observed-by time <= decision; publication time may remain null but cannot be replaced silently. Ambiguous, contradictory or unlinked evidence stays missing. Current game-table QB identities must never supply pregame starter identity. Missing does not mean healthy, unchanged or zero adjustment.

Primary market comparator: latest observed DraftKings full-game two-sided moneyline pair at or before decision, no more than 60 minutes old, same event and source snapshot. Normalize American implied probabilities by their sum. ESPN retrieval time proves observation only, not quote freshness; report it explicitly as an observation-time proxy. GOING archives must have both local archive/availability and source quote time before decision; no in-play rows. Missing benchmark excludes the common comparison sample. Repeat descriptive matching at 30 and 120 minutes, without selecting the best result. The final archived pre-kickoff quote is a later-information diagnostic, never presumed to be the actual closing quote. games.csv closing prices are separately labelled retrospective benchmarks.

Existing 2026 games are descriptive audit only and may not select or fit a challenger. Earlier examined seasons, including 2025, remain spent. Outcomes are attached after sample construction. Ties and unfinished games have no binary score. No historical adjustment is manufactured if provenance is absent.

## One frozen candidate, conditional on adequate future evidence

Form x = home QB downgrade indicator minus away QB downgrade indicator. A downgrade means the QB explicitly represented by the frozen champion is confirmed unavailable and an explicitly identified replacement is confirmed available to start. Zero requires affirmative unchanged-starter evidence for BOTH teams; missing is excluded. This deliberately tests one availability mechanism without estimating retrospective QB quality or selecting player features.

p_new = sigmoid(logit(p_champion) + beta*x). Fix beta in [-2,0], no intercept, no further calibrator, one parameter. Fit mean binary log loss + 0.01*beta^2 with bounded scalar minimization (absolute tolerance 1e-8). Champion calibration and probabilities stay frozen. Preserve evidence for what QB the champion actually encoded; monitoring-only names do not establish this.

Only games whose decision time is after this protocol's commit qualify for future fitting. First 200 fully evidenced games across at least 20 weeks are development, with at least 30 nonzero x and both outcomes. Chronological development: first 100 training, next blocks of 25 predicted using only preceding completed games; require at least 10 nonzero x in the initial training block. Freeze one final beta on the 200 development games before confirmation. This is one configuration, at most four walk-forward fits plus one final fit. If any prerequisite fails, no fit. No recalibration tuning. No fitting or selection on 2026 outcomes; enrollment can begin in 2026, but fitting waits for an explicitly authorized eligible later training cohort. This restriction takes precedence over numerical enrollment targets.

## Metrics, power and stopping

Primary paired loss difference is challenger minus champion log loss, clipped probabilities at [1e-6,1-1e-6]. Brier, five fixed reliability bins, calibration-in-the-large and accuracy are secondary. Same rows for champion, challenger and market; no comparison against champion alone on a larger sample. Bootstrap 2,000 whole-game draws with seed 20261026; report week-block sensitivity when sufficient weeks exist. One primary hypothesis; no family search. Benchmark comparisons are descriptive and cannot replace the primary criterion.

Minimum worthwhile reduction is 0.010 absolute log loss. This is a declared utility threshold, not something power analysis can determine. Planning: two-sided alpha .05, power .80, n=ceil(((1.959964+0.841621)*sigma/0.010)^2), with paired-loss SD scenarios .10/.20/.40. Show detectable loss at the observed sample size. These are planning approximations, not effect estimates; dependence and rare QB changes reduce effective information. Do not estimate challenger variance from an invented challenger. Before opening future confirmation, use development-only paired residual SD to set N=max(400,planned N), at least 30 weeks. If infeasible, stop underpowered rather than lower the threshold.

Accept only on untouched confirmation if the mean paired improvement >=0.010, its 95% CI excludes zero, Brier is no worse, and no timestamp sensitivity reverses direction. Otherwise reject if enough evidence rules out the declared benefit; otherwise inconclusive. No promotion follows automatically. Review at end of 2026 week 18 under the existing program stopping rule; insufficient enrollment means paused/inconclusive, no automatic extension.

## Error attribution and audit controls

Champion-minus-same-time-market and same-time-minus-final-archived-market losses can be computed descriptively. Their sum is an accounting identity, not causal separation of model weakness and late news. Missing news timing, quote revisions and other simultaneous information prevent causal attribution. No inference of betting profit or market efficiency/noise floor. Inspect whether the winner path is stochastic; without repeated same-input forecasts and seeds, numerical simulation noise cannot be estimated from archive differences. Changes in inputs cannot serve as simulation replicates.

Freeze the source inventory at audit start because an existing external scheduler may append files. Hash all consumed files; retain exclusions and audit failures. Test post-decision exclusion, exact boundary, source timestamp conflicts, same-book paired prices, ties, duplicate-game handling and immutable output. Independent reviewer must reproduce selection and primary arithmetic from the frozen packet, not rely on the report. No qualifying data means zero fits and no challenger probability/forecast; archive only a factual blocked-status packet. A prospective forecast must be created before its decision time and kickoff with frozen configuration and training cutoff; never backdate a replay.
