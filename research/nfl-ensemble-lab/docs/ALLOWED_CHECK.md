# Fantasy points allowed to each position, converted to stats: what was tested

Generated by `scripts/allowed_check.py`. Fitted on 2022 to 2024, scored on 2025 and on 2026 so far (weeks 3 to 7) from weeks before each game. Aggregate numbers only.

The baseline is the nflverse-only prediction. Each variant scales it by how far the opposing defense's last six games sit from the league average against that position (pulled toward the league when the record is short). "Stat" uses the yards or catches that defense allowed to the position. "Fantasy points" uses its fantasy-point total instead. Error is the typical miss for one player in one game; lower is better. The interval is 90%. A variant is used only if the whole interval is below zero in both years.
