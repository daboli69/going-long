# Defensive coverage in the simulated game: what was tested

Generated by `scripts/coverage_check.py`. Fitted on 2022 to 2024, scored on 2025 and on 2026 so far (weeks 3 to 7) using only weeks before each game. Aggregate numbers only.

The baseline is the nflverse-only prediction: the player's recent share of the team's total. Each family adjusts it by a few percent depending on the defense faced. Error is the typical miss for one player in one game (root mean squared error); lower is better. The interval is 90%, resampling team-games. A family is used only if the whole interval is below zero in both years.
