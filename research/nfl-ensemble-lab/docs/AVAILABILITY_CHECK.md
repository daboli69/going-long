# Roster absences (injured reserve and others): what they change

Generated by `scripts/availability_check.py`. Every test compares against the old rule, which counts only the official report's "Out". Fits use 2022 to 2024; scores are on 2025 and 2026 games the fit never saw. Aggregate numbers only.

Timing: the official report and the weekly roster are weekly snapshots without time stamps inside the week. Official statuses are treated as known from the Friday report, roster injured reserve, released and retired as known from the start of the week, and "inactive" only on game day.
