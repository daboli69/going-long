# Player rankings: how they matched real results

Generated by `scripts/rankings_check.py` (numbers in `docs/rankings_check.json`). The rankings are built the way the Game Center builds them, with every
number as of before each week, for the played weeks of 2026 from week 3 on (week 8 so far counts only its played game). They are compared with the
real box scores. Aggregate numbers only. This is a description of how well the lists order players. It says nothing about betting prices.

## Yardage and catches

Rank correlation (Spearman) between each player's score and what he did that week, averaged over weeks. The last column is the same measure for the
projection alone.

| List | Player-games | Score | Projection only | Top 10 each week averaged | All players averaged |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Receiving yards | 812 | 0.43 | 0.44 | 75 yards | 36 yards |
| Catches | 844 | 0.45 | 0.44 | 5.9 | 3.2 |
| Rushing yards | 328 | 0.60 | 0.63 | 80 yards | 42 yards |
| Passing yards | 138 | 0.26 | 0.26 | 261 yards | 234 yards |

The lists separate strong weeks from ordinary ones. The blended score (projection, matchup and usage) orders players about as well as the projection alone and
slightly worse for receiving and rushing yards, so matchup and usage are not adding ordering skill on these weeks. The blend was chosen by Travis for a
Venom-style single number; the screen says so.

## Anytime touchdown

Chance of at least one rushing or receiving touchdown, 1,283 player-games. The fifths below split players by predicted chance.

| Fifth of players | Predicted | Actual |
|---|---|---|
| Lowest | 4% | 12% |
| Second | 10% | 15% |
| Middle | 17% | 19% |
| Fourth | 25% | 25% |
| Highest | 45% | 42% |

Overall the model predicted 20% and 23% happened. Likeliest scorers are close to right. Long shots are too low: the lowest fifth scored three times as often as
predicted, because players with small roles still score. A single scale on the expected touchdowns (1.14) would improve the log loss only from 0.521 to 0.519, against 0.539 for
always guessing the average, so no scale is applied. These weeks were also used to read the table, so this is a check on the same data, not a test on new games.

## Limits

- Six weeks of one season. Differences of about 0.02 in rank correlation are noise.
- Completed games use the quarterback who actually started, so weeks 3 to 7 are slightly easier than a forecast made before kickoff.
- Market lines exist only for some players; the "above the line" tags use them where present and have not been checked against results.
