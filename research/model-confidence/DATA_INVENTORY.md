## Paid exports and public evidence

The nine supplied defense/pace CSV types already reach `shared/dfs-matchup-csv.js` and Classic Tournament Research. QB/RB/WR/TE overall positional FPG changes only the optional research-score scenario. Outside WR `(1)`, slot WR `(2)`, receiving alignment, pace and team defense are context. Baseline fantasy projections and betting TD/game probabilities are unchanged. The CSVs are browser-only, expire after 36 hours and require season confirmation; provider fantasy scoring is not verified DraftKings scoring. Their present-day aggregate values cannot be inserted into historical folds as if available then. Need authorized dated exports before evaluating paid-data lift; no inferred receiver alignment or invented coefficients.

Public `football_context.json` provides current team/player scopes, PBP usage, snap and position defense, plus descriptive with/without and opportunity evidence. Inspected 2026 PBP/snaps/rosters are loaded, but **2026 participation is unavailable (`ValueError`)**. Prior-season charting is not current route/coverage measurement. A new model must mark that dimension missing rather than claim full-data confidence. Current injury news can gate future picks but reconstructed weekly reports do not prove historical pregame knowledge.

## Point-in-time risks and mandatory contracts

Current snapshots have build/as-of dates and per-source status; they are not immutable historical feature/price receipts. Completed game scores and revised PBP support a reconstructed score backtest with that limitation, not a prospective validation claim. For every future prediction store model/version, cutoff, immutable input hashes, latest completed game, source checked/observed times, market/side/exact line, paired quote receipt, roster decision and selected slate. Missingness is explicit; recent filesystem/build timestamps cannot rehabilitate stale source contents. Prices must not enter a purported independent football score prediction accidentally.

## Opponent-adjusted ridge: independent challenge

A season-only attack/defense model is a reasonable **Challenger**, not an automatic confidence repair. Approximately four games per NFL team produce sparse, schedule-confounded estimates for roughly 64 attack/defense parameters. Center team effects, regularize strongly, avoid unconstrained home-advantage estimation from a few games and publish shrinkage/sample support. Fit only completed earlier games from the forecast season; freeze penalty/home effect before scoring, or tune solely in earlier chronological folds. Test same-time-game leakage, neutral venues, roster changes, bye weeks, duplicate games, sparse teams, postponements, garbage-time effects, nonnegative score outputs and teams outside the fitted graph.

Compare identical eligible games with raw symmetric current PF/PA, a current league scoring baseline, existing trailing Champion and actual posted main-line benchmark where available. Report MAE/RMSE for **both** margin and total, bias, interval coverage/width and game-clustered paired uncertainty; do not claim better moneyline probabilities from lower score RMSE alone. Model home/away residual dependence before turning two scores into total/margin probabilities. Keep push/tie mass and college overtime rules explicit.

Root's retrospective split (2016–22 development, 2023 diagnostic, 2024–25 reused historical evaluation) must be labeled **locked retrospective**, not untouched holdout. Current team form may be season-only while coefficients/error distributions learn from earlier seasons; explain that distinction. Seal 2026 Week 6+ predictions before kickoff as the prospective promotion gate. User wants useful directional forecasts; beating a market is not required to display an honest experimental lean, but evidence against simple baselines is required before claiming improved accuracy/calibration.

## Phased full-engine acceptance

1. Preserve Champion and eligibility; build immutable feature/outcome receipts and explicit NFL/NCAA missingness map.
2. Validate independent game means and joint residual uncertainty; expose point lean, predictive interval and sample/source quality separately from win chance.
3. Add opportunity→efficiency player Challengers family by family; test feature ablations, current-only versus mixed-history baselines, injury/nonparticipation and sparse roles. Require real outcomes, chronological player/team/game clustering and untouched later predictions.
4. Calibrate market-specific probabilities on training/validation only. Report Brier/log loss, slope/intercept/reliability, continuous error, push/void handling and main/alternate segmentation. Unsupported markets remain explicit research or unavailable.
5. Quantify paid-export incremental value only on reproducible dated licensed inputs; retain device-only privacy unless publication rights are established. Add lineup optimization features only after projection changes pass their gate.

Do not create a numeric confidence score by multiplying available feature counts or widening model/price disagreement. Confidence means measured residual uncertainty and verified data/sample quality; estimated event likelihood is a separate quantity. No model promotion or profitability claim follows from this inventory.
