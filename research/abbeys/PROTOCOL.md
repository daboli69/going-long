## Question and boundaries

Can a current-season-only, opponent-adjusted additive score model improve next-week margin and team-score error over a simple current-season-only scoring average? Candidate outputs are expected home and away scores, an illustrative exact-score pick, and one straight-up pick. Neither method outputs a win probability or calibrated confidence. Show observed games per team and origin week as the evidence label. Exact score is a point forecast, not a likely precise outcome.

For manual v1 output, B is permitted with fewer than three preceding games/team when at least one eligible same-season game supplies a league scoring mean. A team with zero games takes that **current-season** league mean for both offense and defense; label the forecast `Limited evidence`. This is a usability rule, not an eligible observation in the registered B-versus-C comparison below. With zero completed same-season games in the entire league, no evidence-based numerical score exists; a required winner can only be the lexical team-code tie break labeled `No score evidence`. Do not display `0–0` as a forecast or silently import an older season.

For a qualitative cue, refit B after omitting each prior completed week in turn. Label `Limited evidence` when either team's full-data count is below three or any omitted-week refit leaves no league result or fewer than one game for either team. Otherwise label `Stable lean` only if all omitted-week unrounded margins are nonzero and select the same winner as the full-data forecast; label `Sensitive lean` when any differs or ties. These are deterministic sensitivity labels, not outcome probabilities or validated confidence levels. The diagnostic never changes the full-data winner or scores.

Full-data winner is the sign of unrounded expected margin. An exact-zero margin uses the lexically first canonical team code as a deterministic winner and must be labeled `Toss-up; deterministic tie break, no model separation`. Round each expected score to the nearest nonnegative integer. If this produces a tied exact score while the unrounded pick has a winner, choose the nearest nonnegative adjacent integer pair consistent with that winner by minimum squared distance to the two unrounded scores; if two pairs have exactly equal squared distance, choose the pair with the **lower combined point total**. This deterministic display rule keeps the score and winner internally consistent; it does not improve prediction accuracy or create evidence of separation.

Use NFL regular-season weeks 1–18 with valid team IDs, UTC kickoff and two finite scores. No sportsbook field, market line, prior-season game, earlier-year rating, published probability, manual ranking, betting return or player input enters a forecast. Historical 2024–25 seasons are used only for offline method checks; **each historical forecast uses only scores from its own season**. The 2026 forecasts use only 2026 scores. Store neither provider credentials nor private tracker inputs. A revised nflverse snapshot is a retrospective schedule/results source, not point-in-time publication evidence.

Prediction origin is the earliest kickoff of each season/week, minus twelve hours. Use only completed games in **earlier weeks** of that season whose kickoff is at least 12 hours before origin; the delay is an availability precaution, not proof of actual publication. No same-week result, including Thursday's, enters later games that week. Common **B-versus-C evaluation** begins at Week 4 and requires at least three available earlier games for each matchup team. This avoids arbitrary cross-season opening priors in the comparison. Weeks 1–3 and any matchup with insufficient evidence are comparison abstentions, with counts reported; the manual B output rule above is separate. Postponements and ties remain in score-error analysis; actual ties are excluded from winner accuracy. Duplicate IDs or impossible score records fail closed.

The model uses the schedule's designated home team to estimate current-season `h`. No stadium or travel advantage is assumed beyond that observed home score difference. A confirmed neutral-site flag may set the venue contribution to zero; the checked-in snapshot does not provide a reliable neutral flag for this experiment, so neutral-site forecasts must be marked as potentially biased. Weather, injuries, starting quarterbacks and other changing context are absent, unquantified and must not be inferred from scores.

## Frozen candidates

**B, current-season scoring average:** From all available prior games, league points per team `mu = total points/(2*games)`. Team offense is its mean scored points, or `mu` if absent; team defense is its mean allowed points, or `mu` if absent. Current-season home margin `h = mean(home-away)` across available games. `home=(home offense + away defense)/2+h/2`; `away=(away offense + home defense)/2-h/2`. The three-game common gate normally leaves no missing team average. This is a transparent simple benchmark; its observed home edge can be noisy.

**C, opponent-adjusted least squares:** On the same games, fit both team scores with ordinary least squares: `points = mu + venue*h/2 + offense[scoring team] + defense[opponent]`, venue `+1` home and `-1` away. Constrain the sum of offense values to zero and the sum of defense values to zero. Use all prior games with equal weight and no tuned penalty or recency coefficient. Fit by `numpy.linalg.lstsq`; if the design is rank deficient or either team is absent, C abstains. Project both matchup scores with the fitted model. Coefficients are descriptive, not isolated player or coaching effects. Negative model scores, if any, are clipped to zero for forecast and flagged; no other post-fit correction. Compare B and C only on C-eligible games, and report C coverage against all B-eligible games.

**R, recency diagnostic only:** Recompute B with each team's most recent four prior games and the league/home terms from the prior four complete weeks. This is a fixed, interpretable window, not a fitted weight. It is exploratory and cannot displace the registered B-versus-C comparison or justify production recency weighting. Report paired errors against B only after the B/C analysis.

No parameter grid, optimization, or choice based on the 2026 outcomes is allowed. If C coverage is too low, report insufficient evidence; do not relax the rank gate after inspecting errors.

The registered origin is earliest **whole-week** kickoff minus twelve hours, including any Thursday game excluded from the Sunday/Monday user board. An official holdout capture must occur in the 08:00–08:15 ET slot on the calendar date containing that origin and strictly before the origin, with identical schedule/result input hashes for B and C. No backfill or alternate time selection is allowed. The minimum sample gates below may therefore be unattainable; report insufficient evidence if so.

On identical paired games, primary metrics are margin RMSE and combined team-score RMSE (two team scores per game). Secondary: margin/team-score/total MAE, total RMSE, winner accuracy excluding actual ties, rounded exact-score match count, bias and missingness/coverage by week. A zero predicted margin is an abstention for winner scoring. Report denominators. Do not claim probability calibration, ROI or CLV. Compute paired uncertainty by resampling whole week clusters 2,000 times with fixed seed 20261003; report the 95% percentile interval for B RMSE minus C RMSE for each primary metric. Show 2024 and 2025 separately. One season and weekly team dependencies limit inference.

For *future* 2026 consideration only, require at least 200 paired games across 12 weeks, at most 10% C abstentions among B-eligible games, and both primary RMSE improvements at least 0.25 points with both paired 95% week-bootstrap intervals above zero; also no >0.25-point total RMSE deterioration. These are practical screening gates, not claims of optimal thresholds or permission to promote. Any 2025 finding is diagnostic only; a 2026 passing result still needs manual review and Travis's approval for a model change. The currently deployed methods remain Champion. Insufficient sample, failed gates, or missing as-of snapshots mean reject/insufficient, never retrofit a new cutoff.

## Method and provenance references

- [nflverse data releases](https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data) document the public automated schedule/results source. The checked-in snapshot does not retain its upstream release hash or result publication times.
- [Hyndman and Athanasopoulos, time-series cross-validation](https://otexts.com/fpp3/tscv.html) motivates rolling forecast origins, where only earlier observations train a prediction.
- [statsmodels, rank-deficient design warning](https://www.statsmodels.org/stable/pitfalls.html) explains why a generalized inverse can yield numerically available but poorly identified team coefficients. The rank gate is conservative and may reduce early-season coverage.

The additive score model and four-game recency check are hypotheses, not established NFL winners. The offline evaluation can support a score-accuracy comparison only; it cannot establish a calibrated confidence level or real betting value.
