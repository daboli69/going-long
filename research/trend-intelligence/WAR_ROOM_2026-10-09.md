## Rejected (do not rediscover)
- Last-3-vs-last-6 trends for anytime TD (JP-1..5: adds nothing beyond level).
- Last-12 TD totals as a ranking (no better than Champion once the log transform is fair).
- Position dummies for TD markets; injury-created opportunity as a TD feature (SHADOW, CI spans 0).
- Single-phi NB for receptions as written.
- A dead-row gate (DK average points below 3 gets zero ownership) for Classic ownership: per-player MAE 3.03 -> 2.93 but worse on live rows (3.04 -> 4.08) and no change to the lineup-level bias (215 actual vs about 99 shown).
- Lineup-level ownership as a duplication signal for Classic (understates optimizer-built lineups: 53% shown vs 161% actual on one real slate).
- "Leverage" as a validated ROI source in either Showdown or Classic.
