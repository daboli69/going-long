# DFS tournament research v1

Manual session, October 5, 2026. Product: choose **Classic**, **Captain / MVP**, or **Player ideas**; inspect the projection, upside assumption, roster relationships and main risk; import the actual contest salary file before constructing a contest lineup. Keep the approved visual system. Existing Best DFS, Touchdown Throne, betting models, GOING Score and frozen ranking experiments are unchanged.

## What public ETR research supports

[ETR Showdown 101](https://establishtherun.com/nfl-showdown-101/), [Levitan's Milly Maker discussion](https://establishtherun.com/levitan-how-to-win-draftkings-nfl-milly-maker/) and the public [2022 strategy guide](https://cdn.establishtherun.com/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/23110421/2022-DFS-Strategy-Guide.pdf) motivate examining upside, QB/pass-catcher stacks, opponent responses, Captain relationships, salary context and field ownership. Their historical associations are not a validated 2026 GOING policy. No paid ETR projections, ownership, proprietary weights or contest data are copied.

Classic and Showdown require different construction explanations. Stacks, double stacks, bring-backs, team splits, Captain positions and leftover salary are descriptive context, never universal hard constraints. Ownership and duplication remain **unknown** without a real feed. There is no field simulation, contest equity, fitted covariance, winning probability or guaranteed optimal lineup.

## Versioned ordering, not a new projection model

`dfs-tournament-research-v1` ranks current modeled partial fantasy points plus an observed historical scoring-spread proxy. `observed-partial-fantasy-residual-v1` uses complete joint game rows from the current/prior season, completed at least 24 hours before capture, with seven measured fields: passing yards/TDs, rushing yards/TDs, receiving yards/TDs and receptions. Require at least eight rows. Available profiles currently retain at most twelve appearances. Score each actual row using existing platform scoring and actual yardage-bonus thresholds; calculate nearest-rank historical 90th percentile minus historical mean, floored at zero. Add that spread to the current projection.

The result is a **dispersion assumption**, not a forecast percentile or fitted ceiling. Eight rows is a pragmatic coverage minimum, not empirically validated. Prior roles and current-season sample differences matter. Correlated selections invalidate interpreting the sum as a joint lineup tail. Missing/invalid upside falls back to current projection, with coverage disclosed; it is not invented from `projection × constant`. Current mean forecasts are not retuned. Tournament mode adds no arbitrary stack fantasy-point bonuses. Bounded beam search and swaps preserve roster legality but do not guarantee a global optimum. Best DFS retains the mean-points objective; Throne retains its separate team-budget scenario and freshness gate.

Passing interceptions, fumbles, return scores and two-point conversions are absent from the modeled component total. DST/K imported platform averages are historical fallbacks, not GOING matchup forecasts. FanDuel yardage bonuses are retained: [official scoring update](https://www.fanduel.com/research/daily-fantasy-football-what-you-should-know-about-fanduel-s-updated-scoring-for-2024). No assertion of improved forecast accuracy is made from these plumbing repairs.

## Availability and projection plumbing

`dfs-availability-v1` is scoped to DFS. The shared injury adapter is preserved byte-for-byte because it is a registered betting-experiment source. DFS consumes actual `position`, `roster_status` and `injury` fields and lets exact current evidence override the generic all-ACT roster feed. Known INA/RES/DEV/CUT/RET/EXE, spelled-out nonactive statuses, OUT/IR and doubtful designations are excluded before model or imported-average fallback. The [NFLverse roster-status dictionary](https://nflreadr.nflverse.com/articles/dictionary_roster_status.html) supplies code meanings. Questionable/limited practice is disclosed, not called confirmed OUT; unknown codes remain unknown. Last-known unavailability does not silently become active when old. A generated roster is not final game-day inactive confirmation.

Quarterbacks require a fresh, unambiguous current depth role, excluding unavailable quarterbacks. No alphabetical starter inference. Projection-only ideas require recent/current participation; zero current targets/carries do not generate skill-player ideas. Strict Classic retains current-season opportunity and all four QB/five skill core components. Exact player/team/position, opponent and kickoff must match before reusing an existing projection or blending a quote; otherwise model the imported game or disclose the imported-average fallback. Any component's blocking injury excludes the player. Started games cannot generate new ideas. An unavailable existing lock stays visible with an actionable conflict; it is never silently replaced.

## Prospective records and validation

Voluntary **Freeze pregame lineup** stores a separate local DFS journal (`dfs-lineup-receipt-1`), never betting ROI or Today-ranking observations. Record actual process time, SHA-256 of imported salary bytes, contest/site, exact slots/IDs/salaries/kickoffs, projections, source-tagged upside/sample, opportunity/model/availability evidence and strategy versions. Require a complete legal roster, genuine imported-file identity, future kickoffs and valid source clocks. CPT projection multiplier is 1.5; an already priced CPT salary is charged once. Storage appends without rewriting old receipts, rejects corrupt/duplicate records and stops at capacity. Export is available. Records are device-local and voluntary, with selection bias; no automatic results, prize/equity/ROI or population coverage is claimed.

Future hypothesis, declared before any prospective analysis: on identical frozen pools/locks/caps, this fixed spread ordering produces greater upper-tail realized partial fantasy points than Best DFS without sacrificing legality. First collect a separate paired cohort across at least 30 independent slates, recording both strategies before first kickoff and source failures. Keep collection/diagnostic and subsequent untouched holdout slates chronological. Evaluate mean/error and upper-tail outcomes with slate-cluster uncertainty, sample/coverage and loss cases; keep genuine field/prize/ownership outcomes separate. Thirty slates is a preliminary research minimum, not a guarantee of adequate power. Do not tune v1 from voluntary winners or claim superiority from eight-player model-only cores. Preregister any fitted replacement and its cutoff/splits/criteria separately; Model Gate and promotion approval remain binding.

Remaining gaps: validated joint player/game outcomes, reliable ownership/field duplication, full scoring, current routes/first reads, defensible expected fantasy points, as-of injury/with-without effects, current official contest salaries and prospective contest results. Public play-by-play/current usage can support future work, but missing inputs are not fabricated. This release implements useful research ordering and explanations, not an ETR-equivalent simulator.
