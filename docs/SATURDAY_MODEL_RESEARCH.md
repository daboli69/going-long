## 5. Optional five-entry portfolio research

The present engine maximizes each slip's estimated win probability. A distinct proposed objective is `P(S1 union ... union S5)`. Shared-player overlap invalidates treating slips independent even when each slip uses three different games. Under explicitly assumed independent **player** events, an intersection of slips is the product over the union of their unique players. Inclusion-exclusion then computes the portfolio union exactly. `portfolio.py` implements this, plus exact evaluation under a caller-supplied finite joint outcome law and exhaustive optimization of a bounded candidate pool (<=24). Real candidate legality/price terms must still be supplied by the existing engine; this research does not substitute fabricated odds or weaken eligibility.

Independent truth-table enumeration confirms inclusion-exclusion to12decimal places over80seeded five-slip pools. Shared A in AB/AC at .5 each gives .375, while the erroneous independent-slip expression gives .4375. Duplicate slips add no coverage; a weaker disjoint slip can reduce the selected portfolio's probability relative to a stronger overlapping alternative. Synthetic perfect-dependence scenarios also change the optimum. These are mathematical/synthetic checks, not historical TD validation.

No real five-entry union estimate is recommended: marginal TD calibration and within-game joint law are unverified; cross-slip players can share games. A Gaussian/copula guess or arbitrary correlation coefficient would manufacture evidence. This objective also ignores shared prize, multiple winners, refunds and wager return; those may make “at least one” different from the user's true payoff objective. Keep existing ranking; later expose only an explicitly selected alternative after dated joint-scenario validation and Travis approval. No automatic diversification preference.
