# GOING Confidence: evidence readiness

Today -> choose NFL/NCAA and date -> GOING Confidence. This descriptive presentation reviews existing eligible contracts; it does not replace Today ranking, estimate calibrated confidence, use GOING Score, or promote a betting model.

- **Current support attached:** a traceable current-season NFL receiving-role or exact player/opponent/role/direction defensive observation supports the contract, current coverage exists, and no sample/availability/quote check blocks it. Observations can overlap; this is not independent confirmation or demonstrated predictive accuracy.
- **Developing evidence:** an eligible existing estimate needs stronger relevant football support. NCAA game models currently have no verified personnel/matchup layer and cannot earn Current support merely from scoring history.
- **Check first:** stale/missing/future price or model evidence, unresolved availability, or a blocking model flag. No supported call is invented to populate the first view.

Cards show the actual contract, game, why to investigate, biggest concern, estimated likelihood, recorded price and win-only break-even. Push-aware estimated return remains pWin * decimalOdds + pPush - 1, not proven value. Deeper evidence shows current/older counts, existing weights, source/cutoff timestamps and missing checks. High chance, attractive payout, model/market disagreement or old charting alone cannot establish current support. Quote <=5 minutes, model/context <=24 hours and latest NFL player observation <=28 days are operational freshness checks, not validated accuracy thresholds.

Current support requires current football-season identity (January belongs to the preceding season), verified direction-relevant inputs and the underlying model's sample-quality flag. Receiving-role support is restricted to Over receiving yards/receptions. Defensive support is restricted to the exact receiving/rushing-yards role, player, opponent, kickoff and weakness lineage. Other market evidence stays descriptive. NCAA has game markets only. All views preserve existing Today order and date/game/book filters; pagination exposes the entire eligible view, not a permanent Top Five.

NFL and NCAA probabilities, price calculations, game-first navigation, GOING Score, Jackpot and Parlay Lab are unchanged. The one small complementary workflow is the actionable evidence checklist and exact-contract Compare/parlay saving. At phone width the large football tab block scrolls away while the GOING header remains accessible; keyboard focus and expanded evidence survive ordinary view/pagination changes.

## NCAA research boundary
Current production already assigns 80% current / 20% older history whenever both exist, over a trailing twelve-game window with five combined games required. This is an unvalidated Champion policy, not a product requirement. Expose the current team sample and older fallback honestly; never relabel old team observations as current because a file was rebuilt.

`research/ncaa-recency/README.md` and `protocol.json` audit the actual pipeline and preregister a separate window/decay/shrinkage experiment. Historical as-of inputs are missing: no fit, winner or production weighting change. Learn appropriate feature-specific windows from chronological evidence; do not prescribe 80/20, infer coaching/QB/roster continuity, reuse NFL conclusions for NCAA, or change production models without approval.

Keep today-ranking-v1 policy/generator/C1/C2 definitions and immutable observations intact. Confidence is not a new experimental policy or a new performance cohort. New ranking/feature hypotheses require a separately versioned future registration; do not tune existing policies after results.
