## 1–9. What Confidence means and where to find it

1. **Meaning:** GOING Confidence is qualitative evidence readiness for an exact available betting contract. It helps answer what supports investigating it, what needs checking and whether the saved price is usable. It is deliberately honest when nothing meets the support checks.
2. **Not:** a calibrated win percentage, guaranteed winner, proven betting edge, GOING Score, a newly validated universal bet ranking, or an empirical claim that a higher evidence tier wins more.
3. **NFL entry:** Football -> NFL -> Today -> prominent GOING Confidence, directly after the date/slate control. An initial shortcut jumps to the section without navigating to the home page. Game-first research remains immediately below.
4. **NCAA entry:** Football -> NCAA -> Today -> the same section, with NCAA game markets only. No NCAA player props are invented.
5. **NFL method:** reviews the existing filtered Today probability-ranked contract pool. Current support requires current-season model coverage and a verified direction-relevant receiving-role or exact player/opponent/role/kickoff defensive signal, operationally current source/price timestamps, no unresolved model/availability checks and no low player-sample flag. The receiving-role rule is restricted to Over receiving yards/receptions; defensive residuals to their exact receiving/rushing-yards role. Inactive context, wrong market/direction, old charting, price or probability alone cannot qualify.
6. **NCAA method:** current/older team samples and actual weighting are exposed. This data has score models but no verified current personnel/matchup layer, so scoring history alone cannot earn Current support. With fresh prices it may be Developing; stale/invalid evidence is Check first. NCAA and NFL are independently treated.
7. **Increasing support:** traceable current exact-contract role/matchup observations, correct season, relevant direction, usable source cutoffs and availability checks. These are overlapping observations, not independent confirmations. No arbitrary numerical total or hand-tuned confidence weight is introduced.
8. **Decreasing support:** limited/current-missing samples, historical participation, unresolved injury/replacement role, missing/future/stale sources, unavailable current matchup support, short prices or non-positive estimated return. Five-minute quotes, 24-hour model/context and 28-day player-observation cutoffs are operational checks, not empirically optimal accuracy thresholds.
9. **Three separate concepts:** likelihood is the existing model estimate; evidence readiness is the qualitative view; price is the recorded book line/odds, win-only break-even and push-aware estimated return. The card explicitly labels return as unproven value and warns that saved prices may no longer exist. GOING Score stays a player index and is not used here. Current support/Developing/Check first retain existing Today order within each view; all eligible entries are paginated, never a permanent Top Five.
