# Players to Target (Charts, first tab)

Code: `shared/going-targets.js` (pure logic, `tests/going-targets.test.cjs`), `shared/going-targets-ui.js` (UI), wired in `index.html` and `shared/going-charts-ui.js`. No new data source: it reads `history.json` role trends and scoring roles, `data/charts/opportunity_vs_production.json`, `data/going_score.json`, `data/injury_context.json`, `data/nfl_roster.json` and the live prop board.

## Availability first
A player is never actionable (fantasy or betting) when: roster status is RES/INA/PUP/IR/SUS/RET/EXE; the team is on a bye; practice squad/development/cut; blank or missing roster status ("unconfirmed", not treated as active); no appearance in 21 days; current-week game status Out/Inactive/Doubtful. Questionable, game-time decision, did-not-practice and limited-practice keep the player with a visible caution. A reserve designation is never assumed season-ending (minimum four games); return dates are not in our data, so returning players appear only if `expected_return_week` is ever published, and then only as a labelled stash-only list. Game-day inactives come about 90 minutes before kickoff and are not in the feed; the page says so and shows the snapshot timestamp. The roster snapshot fills teams missing from the injury file (bye teams).

## Fantasy categories (fixed design rules, not fitted)
Buy low: season (or last-3) points at least 2.5/game below expected points, top-40% opportunity at the position, role not shrinking, 3+ games. Descriptive only: the opportunity-vs-production gap did not predict later points (E12). Emerging: role growing over last 3 vs last 6 games, GOING Score at least 40, top-40% opportunity. Waiver/stash: growing role, modest opportunity. Sell high/avoid: 3+ points/game above expected with modest opportunity or a shrinking role. Monitor: high opportunity with an injury caution.

## Betting targets
Research targets, not edges. Direction follows the role trend (growing: Over; shrinking: Under; flat: no target; anytime TD only for PRIMARY/SECONDARY scoring roles). The priced offer is the one nearest the main line of the posted ladder; alternate and extreme lines are not shown. Value is the de-vigged price with a 15% model weight (live receptions test: the price predicted outcomes with slope 1.0, the model added nothing detectable, weight about -0.02, upper bound about 0.26); one-sided prices (anytime TD) show no value. Model-only value is displayed beside it, withheld when the model gives 85%+ or the model-only EV exceeds 25%. Calibration status is stated per market.

## Betting targets: three tiers (corrected 2026-10-10)
Each card separates (1) the opportunity signal and what was tested, (2) the price check, (3) the recommendation, which is always "research only" because no market has a validated edge against sportsbook prices. Direction is offered only where role information beat GOING's own Champion mean on PARTICIPANTS (books void non-participants): rushing yards on carry share (+16%, 95% interval +1% to +34%, 4 of 5 seasons, weak) and anytime TD on scoring-role tier (log loss -4.2%). RETRACTED: receptions on target share. The earlier +5.6% came from zero-filled did-not-play rows; on participants it is +1.6% (-3.8% to +7.3%), a null. Receiving yards gets no direction: a rising target share predicted fewer yards (-11.8%, -17.7% to -5.6%, negative in all five seasons). Nothing here tests information beyond a sportsbook price.
