The main classification choice to revisit is the **single hard five-minute quote gate**. The ranking page itself admits saved quotes through 24 hours for model research, and its card copy already says saved prices need verification. The five-minute rule is sensible for claims of live price confirmation, but it guarantees Check first for all entries whenever the local odds refresh is late, even when model/availability checks are otherwise clear. Price confirmation should remain explicit and should not be confused with a model-integrity failure. Thin season samples, missing role evidence, and absent NCAA personnel signals are separate evidence limits; do not upgrade them to support without a frozen, exact-identity signal and appropriate sample criteria.
