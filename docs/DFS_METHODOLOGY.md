# NFL DFS evidence and methodology

Audit: 2026-10-03, application/data base `4e5a165`. DFS is a separate product/research area; Champion/C1/C2, GOING Score and their prospective observations are unchanged. This document distinguishes available inputs from supported inference. Unit tests establish software behavior, not calibrated predictions or profitability.

## Contest and salary boundary

Official DraftKings CSV import is the $0 workflow. Use the player list/template for the exact selected NFL Classic contest/draft group; inspect its games against the contest Depth Chart. Preserve imported player ID, name, position, team, opponent, game/start time and salary. No salary estimation, cross-slate merge or account scraping. A CSV is user-supplied official-source evidence, not independent authentication of its origin. Contest title, date, draft group and file identity should remain visible.

DraftKings' [Classic overview](https://support.draftkings.com/dk/en-us/game-style-classic-overview?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0010665) establishes the $50,000 cap and points users to the selected contest Depth Chart for its game set. Its generic description requires at least two teams; this product additionally enforces Travis's stricter at-least-two-games requirement. [CSV help](https://help.draftkings.com/hc/en-us/articles/4405223998867-How-do-I-upload-or-edit-multiple-lineups-at-once-US) describes a template selected by sport, format and start time, with one template per imported lineup file.

Public searches and official rules/help inspection did **not** verify the exact October 4, 2026 Millionaire player pool, salaries or Touchdown Throne offer terms. The supplied 8+ rushing/receiving TD, $25,000 equal-share offer is a user-provided requirement. Do not claim DraftKings has independently confirmed that offer, opt-in, eligibility or qualifying game set. No actual recommended lineup can be certified until the matching official salary file is imported and its selected slate is confirmed.

## DFS points and salary optimization

Reuse existing relevant ready stat projections and the DraftKings scoring coefficients: passing yards .04, passing TD 4, rush/receiving yards .1, rush/receiving TD 6, reception 1, with expected 3-point yardage bonuses from the existing yardage distributions. See the [DraftKings-owned DFS scoring guide](https://dknetwork.draftkings.com/2025/08/27/nfl-dfs-beginners-guide-draftkings/). That guide also lists negative interception/fumble scoring, conversions, returns/recovery TDs and DST scoring. GOING lacks reliable complete projections for these additional channels; its offensive estimate must be labeled partial rather than a complete calibrated DKFP forecast. Existing market-center blending is a heuristic already present in DFS, not a newly validated estimator.

Best mode maximizes summed available DFS point estimates under actual roster, salary, uniqueness, game and pool constraints. The linear sum of expected points does not require players to be independent. Salary efficiency is points per imported $1,000 plus comparison of feasible complete lineups, not a bonus for being cheap. Require actual current playing opportunity for default offensive eligibility; exclude weak/missing role evidence rather than fitting an inactive player into remaining salary. DST averages can compare salary efficiency but do not establish matchup skill.

Beam search is approximate; no global-optimum claim. Feasible pruning/search should retain affordable candidates and lock feasibility, deduplicate interchangeable slots, and validate completed outputs. Lock/exclude/swap/rebuild use the same final validator, including two distinct games. A direct swap preserves unaffected slots and tests the resulting complete lineup, showing actual salary/point/TD deltas. Unavailable locks fail with an actionable explanation rather than quietly replacing the user choice.

This repairs access and roster/salary handling, not the projection or Touchdown Throne methods. Captain research remains separate from the Classic eight-rushing/receiving-TD promotion. Automated regressions use explicitly synthetic salary fixtures, not verified real contest lineups. A real official export is still required for a recommendation for an actual contest.

## Touchdown Throne and multi-TD representation

Promo mean per offensive player is `E[rush TD] + E[receiving TD]`. Passing TDs affect QB fantasy points only. A QB rushing or receiving TD contributes once; a pass to a rostered receiver contributes the receiver's TD once. DST contributes zero promotional TDs under the supplied requirement. Returns and recovery TDs also remain excluded pending actual official terms.

The initial 02f8b88 implementation ranked summed qualifying-TD means. This manual update adds the separately versioned, explicitly experimental team-budget-v1 scenario: rank complete legal lineups by their computed eight-plus qualifying-TD scenario mass, then partial DFS points, lower salary and deterministic player-ID ties. The scenario permits one player to score multiple TDs and does not use arbitrary score weights. It is an **uncalibrated threshold scenario objective**, not a validated real-world chance of reaching eight or winning a tournament. Mean-only ranking remains an offline baseline; the public Throne builder never silently falls back when its scenario evidence is missing. An optional explicit competitiveness constraint can compare against Best's attainable point estimate; any chosen threshold is a visible product preference, not a fitted parameter.

Existing player count models can illustrate 0/1/2/3+ scenarios. For a combined marginal Poisson scenario with `lambda = rushMean + recMean`, `p0=exp(-lambda)`, `p1=lambda*p0`, `p2=lambda*p1/2`, `p3plus=1-p0-p1-p2`; see [NIST's Poisson definition](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/handbook/eda/section3/eda366j.htm). This assumes a marginal Poisson count for that player's combined outcomes; independence of his rush/receiving processes is a modeling simplification, not measured evidence. Label estimates unvalidated and round coarsely. A safer companion is raw observed counts of complete player games in those buckets, with current/prior sample sizes and dates. These retrospective frequencies are descriptions, not calibrated current probabilities.

Never calculate/display a lineup P(8+) by convolving nine independent slots. Team scoring opportunities, game script and receiver competition create dependence; passing/receiving fantasy scoring shares events. Existing `correlation()` ceiling/stack bonuses contain heuristic numeric offsets and are not measured correlations; do not reuse them as evidence for these new objectives. Show actual QB/pass-catcher stacks, opponent bring-backs, team/game concentration and DST-opposing-offense conflicts; expose construction preferences without inventing covariance. A teammate concentration warning is useful even though expectation addition remains mathematically valid. No full-lineup scenario is currently validated. The new finite-team allocation is a transparent experimental construction, with independent teams explicitly disclosed; it is not a fitted covariance model.

## Validation and next experiments

No DFS-specific MAE/RMSE, TD/multi-TD calibration, historical optimized-lineup distribution, threshold frequency or profitability result is established. Saved revised player outcomes permit feasibility/dispersion/bucket diagnostics only. Historical optimizer evaluation additionally requires archived **as-of** projections, official slate IDs/player pools/salaries, availability and complete DK-scored outcomes; these are absent. New prospective DFS capture must remain separate from Champion/C1/C2. Freeze inputs before lock, register chronological folds/untouched holdout and compare identical pools against simple/market benchmarks with date/game clustering; never optimize using that slate's realized outcome.

Prioritize (1) frozen real-slate result tracking and partial-projection error/calibration, (2) empirical Poisson-versus-overdispersed count diagnostics with honest uncertainty, (3) team-level TD allocation models preserving finite opportunity, and (4) covariance-aware lineup simulation after joint outcome/as-of availability support. Gate model promotion separately.

Late swap is dependency-gated: [official guidance](https://support.draftkings.com/dk/en-us/how-do-i-late-swap-a-player?id=kb_article_view&sysparm_article=KB0010830) says only supported contests allow swapping before individual game lock, and every resulting lineup must remain legal. Reliable implementation needs actual contest lock metadata, persisted exact lineup slots/started-game locks, trustworthy live kickoff/status and updated final availability. Weekly snapshots alone do not supply this. Keep late swap a documented roadmap dependency until these facts are reliable.

Useful product ideas to retain: evidence-qualified salary values/injury-created role watch; two-lineup comparison with exact salary/points/TD/exposure changes; replacement/upgrade finder using valid remaining salary; and frozen lineup result tracking. Build these around the simple generate/lock/exclude/swap experience; defer ownership claims, arbitrary risk scores and extra contest formats.

## Experimental eight-TD scenario v1 — October 3 manual update

**Definition, frozen before prospective DFS evaluation:** `shared/dfs-threshold.js` uses each team's equally weighted observed current-season regular-season rushing+receiving TD counts, including all scorer positions and overtime. For team t, let its observed budgets be N_t; let D_t = max(mean(N_t), sum of all uniquely verified current-role ready rush+rec player means). Each selected player's share is its existing qualifying-TD mean / D_t. The remaining share belongs to unselected/unmodeled scorers; imported/selected/excluded subsets never determine the denominator. All-zero budgets/means use a division guard of one but allocate zero selected TDs.

Conditional on a finite team budget N_t, TD allocation is multinomial across players/other scorers; the selected cohort's marginal is Binomial(N_t, sum selected shares). This permits 0/1/2/3+ TDs per player and represents competition for finite team scores. See [NIST multinomial definition](https://www.itl.nist.gov/div898/software/dataplot/refman2/auxillar/multpdf.htm). Average those conditional distributions over the actual observed budgets; convolve team distributions with an absorbing 8+ bucket. Different teams, including opponents, are assumed independent: shared game scripts and opponent covariance are not fitted. **This mathematical scenario is not empirically calibrated.** Do not display its internal tail mass as a precise success percentage. QB passing and DST/return TDs never enter qualifying counts; DFS projections stay separate.

The actual public [NFLverse team summary release](https://github.com/nflverse/nflverse-data/releases/tag/stats_team), `stats_team_week_2026.csv`, supplies 98 team-game rows / 49 completed games / 32 teams, only 3–4 games per team. Provider release timestamp: 2026-10-03T14:22:21Z; saved-file retrieval: 18:22:21Z. Raw SHA256 `32f2748b9e482cdab33f11c3d75438872ae98351665956028d25581f011ce9e2`. `research/dfs/team-stats-2026.csv` and the immutable 49-game `results-team-reference.json` permit exact offline reproduction with `build-team-budgets.py`; the generated `data/dfs_team_touchdowns.json` contains source/clock/hash receipts and rejects incomplete, duplicate, future, mismatched and impossible counts. GOING's position-filtered usage export is **not** a complete scorer census: it misses certain positions/overtime (including a known September 27 Deebo receiving TD). It is not used as the team budget.

At import/rebuild, require current-season verified role, model, roster and source clocks, all within 36 hours, and exact identity/TD-mean parity. Invalid/missing team evidence blocks Throne with an explanation; Best DFS remains a separate point objective. Team inputs are currently a deployed static receipt, **not an automatic live provider feed**. Future autonomous cycles may explicitly refresh the official free source, regenerate its reference/artifact, test and publish through the normal application path. Do not call a data-only skipped deployment a refreshed static artifact; no workflow/provider/credentials changes are made here.

The bounded salary-diverse beam first supplies feasible complete seeds; full-roster threshold ranking and up to three single-slot ascent passes on three seeds prioritize 8+ mass, with points/lower-salary/IDs as ties. Swaps compare the resulting complete lineup's threshold scenario while retaining eight slots. Global optimality is not guaranteed. The initial legacy optimizer's score-only cutoffs/beam discarded affordable valid players; it now retains cheap candidates, uses an admissible optimistic remaining-salary bound and reserves cheaper salary bands. Salary limits/locks remain mandatory; genuine infeasible pools still fail.
