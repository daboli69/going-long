# Tonight’s Bets

Presentation-only discovery board on Today. Selects unstarted games on the current Eastern calendar date from the loaded schedule and assessed GOING Picks rows. It does not replace Champion, change probabilities/thresholds, retune models, or alter frozen prospective studies.

## Selection and prices

Reuses the existing matched-candidate and football-case assessment. Current shared eligibility is checked again for player rows at render and save time. Supported spread/total leans lead the default mix; player and TD research remain separate. Model sample count is labeled separately from current-season evidence. Same-line comparisons require the selected game, player, market, side and line; opposite directions and different lines are not interchangeable offers. For each book its newest observation wins. Recent (five-minute) quotes precede saved quotes, then decimal payout orders comparable offers. Prices shorter than -500, invalid/future quotes and observations older than 24 hours are excluded.

Saved prices are research context, visibly marked for rechecking and cannot be saved through this board as current-price selections. Save rechecks the current exact quote, participation and existing evidence, stores only in the existing browser-local comparison list, and sends nothing to sportsbooks. Saved-selection review flags lost/changed quotes and shared-game exposure without inventing joint probabilities. Existing live-refresh mechanism supplies updates; this feature adds no provider calls.

## Verification / rollback

Focused tests cover ET boundaries, kickoff, exact-offer selection, freshness/extreme prices, availability, escaped markup, refresh/focus persistence and saved-selection warnings. Browser smoke uses actual public saved Bills–Rams snapshots, explicitly distinguished from synthetic fresh-price test fixtures. Full-suite/build and exact release receipts reside in ignored `.cache/tonight-bets/`. Rollback: revert the Tonight’s Bets release; existing Today/Markets/Games remain intact.
