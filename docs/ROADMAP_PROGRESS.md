- **P2.1b, chronological role diagnostic:** the passing-snap role rule is now checked week by week using only earlier games for its feature calculation. In the 2025 research season, 191 future player-games tagged “role ahead of results” finished 4.3 receiving yards per game above each player's earlier-game average; the full eligible group finished 0.2 yards above. The 4.1-yard difference from the full group has a week-resampled range of -0.5 to +9.5 yards, which includes no difference. This is a mechanism check, not a betting win rate: the source is a revised public dataset rather than saved weekly vintages, and next-game participation is known after the fact. The signal remains experimental and does not change projections or suggested bets. The UI exposes the result and limitation. Reproducible code and aggregate output are committed; detailed player-game records stay in the ignored private audit output so they do not add nearly a megabyte to every app load.
