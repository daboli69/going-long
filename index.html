How to read the numbers

Estimated return means how far ahead or behind the model expects $1 bets to finish over many repeats. It can be wrong. Book prices and historical averages are different kinds of evidence.

Checking current prices and available evidence…

What is available right now?

Price comparison and historical-model disagreement are available with fresh matched quotes. Role, pace and pressure need observations within 45 days plus enough measured plays. Older observations stay visible in model details but do not establish a current role.

True routes, current injuries, receiver alignment, drops/luck, career milestones and weekly advanced-stat trends are not supported here yet. Opportunity research includes dated position-based defensive comparisons. Pass-snap charting is credited to FTN Data via nflverse; it is not route tracking. Missing charting never counts as zero.

Opportunity & coaching research

Two-leg ideas

Full-game selections at the same book, from different games. First TD, period baselines and flagged model checks are excluded. The combined chance assumes the legs do not affect each other; shared model errors can still affect both. Payouts below multiply single prices and are illustrations, not bookmaker quotes. Check the actual ticket. Same-game and pick’em builds need separate payout and joint-outcome models, so they are not priced here.

Historical estimates assume the player participates. Individual stakes do not account for correlated bets. Yardage: lognormal; receptions and TDs: Poisson. Win probability excludes pushes.

Paste props

One per line, tab or comma separated:
player, team, opp, market, line, overOdds, underOdds, projMean, projSd, kickoffISO
Leave mean and SD blank to match a historical profile. Markets: pass_yds, rush_yds, rec_yds, receptions, pass_tds, rush_tds, rec_tds, atd.
For ATD rows: player, team, opp, atd, , atdOdds, , eTD, , kickoffISO — leave line/underOdds/projSd blank, put λ (expected TDs) in the projMean slot.

Paste game lines

One per line: away, home, spread(home), total, mlAway, mlHome, kickoffISO
Spread is the home team's number (negative = home favored). Team projections match the exact teams and kickoff from the historical pipeline.
