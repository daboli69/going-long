Betting

Manual/paste entry only — no live odds feed is wired in yet. Everything below runs on whatever you paste in.

Paste props

One per line, tab or comma separated:
player, team, opp, market, line, overOdds, underOdds, projMean, projSd, kickoffISO
For ATD rows: player, team, opp, atd, , atdOdds, , eTD, , kickoffISO — leave line/underOdds/projSd blank, put λ (expected TDs) in the projMean slot.

Paste game lines

One per line: away, home, spread(home), total, mlAway, mlHome, kickoffISO[, awayRating, homeRating]
Spread is the home team's number (negative = home favored). Ratings are optional — omit them and the fair-line estimator just won't show anything for that game.
